About SMAK 7

Apa itu SMAK 7?

SMAK 7 PENABUR adalah sekolah berbasis teknologi yang berkomitmen untuk mengembangkan siswa menjadi pribadi yang unggul, inovatif, dan berintegritas dalam menghadapi masa depan digital.

Sejarah SMAK 7

SMAK 7 PENABUR didirikan dengan tujuan memberikan pendidikan berkualitas tinggi yang berfokus pada perkembangan teknologi dan karakter siswa secara menyeluruh.

Visi & Misi

Visi: Menjadi sekolah unggulan dalam bidang teknologi dan karakter.

Misi: Mengembangkan potensi siswa melalui pendidikan berbasis inovasi, teknologi, dan nilai-nilai moral.

Our Programs

The Duke of Edinburgh’s International Award (DofE)

A globally recognized program that builds resilience, leadership, and independence through community service, skill development, physical activities, and outdoor expeditions.

Character Camp

A transformative camp experience focused on leadership, teamwork, and personal growth through outdoor challenges, group activities, and reflection.

Immersion Program

International learning experiences where students explore global cultures, visit institutions, and gain real-world insights beyond the classroom.

Student Activities

Fortelations

Fortelations is SMAK 7’s annual school competition event, bringing students together through sports, games, and team challenges. It fosters school spirit, collaboration, and healthy competition in a fun and energetic environment.

Extracurricular Activities

Students can explore their passions through a wide range of extracurriculars, including sports, arts, technology clubs, and academic organizations, helping them grow beyond the classroom.

Peer Educator

The Peer Educator program empowers students to support and guide their peers through mentorship, leadership initiatives, and awareness programs, promoting a positive and supportive school community.
